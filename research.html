Research

Overview

My research lies in the analysis of partial differential equations, with a focus on inverse problems and related questions in integral geometry and microlocal analysis.

I am particularly interested in recovering coefficients, potentials, and physical parameters from indirect measurements, such as boundary data and scattering data.

Applications motivating these questions include medical imaging, geophysics, and wave scattering.

Research interests

  • Inverse boundary value problems for higher-order elliptic operators, including biharmonic and polyharmonic operators.
  • Partial data inverse problems and the recovery of anisotropic perturbations.
  • Ray transforms and momentum ray transforms on tensor fields.
  • Inverse problems for fractional and nonlocal operators.
  • Nonlinear inverse problems for partial differential equations.
  • Recovery of physical parameters in acoustic, elastic, and acoustic-gravitational systems.

Guiding questions

  • To what extent do boundary or scattering measurements determine the coefficients of an operator?
  • What can be recovered when measurements are available only on part of the boundary?
  • How do nonlinearities, higher-order terms, and nonlocal effects influence an inverse problem?
  • How can integral geometry and microlocal analysis contribute to uniqueness and parameter recovery?
