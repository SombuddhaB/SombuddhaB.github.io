Research

My research concerns inverse problems for partial differential equations and related questions in integral geometry and microlocal analysis. I study the recovery of coefficients and physical parameters from indirect measurements.

Research themes

  • Inverse boundary value problems for higher-order elliptic operators, including biharmonic and polyharmonic operators.
  • Partial data inverse problems and anisotropic perturbations.
  • Ray transforms and momentum ray transforms on tensor fields.
  • Fractional and nonlocal operators.
  • Parameter recovery in acoustic, elastic, and wave propagation models.
